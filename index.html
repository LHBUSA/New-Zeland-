Coverage semantics

If we don't know, we don't pretend we know.

The PropData contract preserves resolution and coverage state because a missing field, an unmatched property and an unavailable source layer are not the same thing.

Are the parcel, address and title baselines national?

Yes. The current LINZ baselines for primary parcels, authoritative addresses, property titles and title↔parcel associations are completed nationally and refresh through PropData ingestion.

Are all 3.268M building outlines loaded?

Not yet. The LINZ building-outline baseline is actively loading. Building depth is therefore a separate coverage layer and should be treated explicitly until the national target is complete and graph linkage is promoted.

Does title data mean named ownership?

No. The current title contract includes title metadata and relationship context. We do not imply named-owner identity unless an authoritative/licensed source specifically supports that field.

Can we expand beyond New Zealand?

Yes. The NZ product is a country-specific commercial surface of the broader PropData global property intelligence platform.
